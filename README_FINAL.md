## Regulatory data policy
Official authority material is the primary regulatory source. Internal commercial/reference documents may have informed planning ranges during research, but no company prices, commercial quotations, or government fees are published on the public website.

## Important
Regulatory requirements can change by product, technology, frequency, model and filing route. The country pages are professional planning references and do not replace a competent authority's final determination.
